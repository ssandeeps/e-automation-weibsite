Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is e-automation an authorized distributor for Allen-Bradley, Siemens, ABB or other brands?

No. e-automation is an independent supplier and sourcing partner for industrial automation parts. We are not an authorized distributor, dealer or representative of Allen-Bradley, Rockwell Automation, Siemens, ABB, Schneider Electric, Honeywell, or any other manufacturer named on this site. We source genuine, tested new, reconditioned and obsolete parts from the open market and our supplier network. All brand names and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

Does e-automation ship parts nationwide across the USA?

Yes. We're headquartered in Folsom, California, and supply industrial automation and control parts to customers across all 50 states, as well as worldwide.

Can you source obsolete or discontinued automation parts?

Yes. Alongside current stock, we specialize in sourcing hard-to-find, obsolete and discontinued parts through our supplier network — PLCs, drives, servo motors, HMIs and safety components no longer available direct from the original manufacturer.

How quickly can you quote on a part?

Typical quotes on stocked lines are turned around within 48 hours. Obsolete or hard-to-find parts may take longer while we search our supplier network — we'll always give you a realistic timeline upfront.
